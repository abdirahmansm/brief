# Brief One-Page Presentation Cheat Sheet

## Demo Goal

Show that Brief turns raw market data into a launch-ready strategy in minutes.

## Live App Status

- Running at: http://localhost:3000
- Frontend + backend are served together by Next.js

## 30-Second Product Positioning

Brief is an AI market research assistant.
It runs multi-step deep analysis, then returns:

- market sizing
- customer segments
- demand confidence
- competitive landscape
- whitespace opportunities
- regulatory risk
- launch strategy

## Best Command Sequence (Use This Order)

1. /market sizing <topic>
2. /market segments <topic>
3. /market demand <topic>
4. /market landscape <topic>
5. /market whitespace <topic>
6. /market regulatory <topic>
7. /market launch <product>
8. /market report <url or target>

## Example Niche

AI accounting software for SMBs in North America

## Exact Demo Inputs

1. /market sizing AI accounting software for SMBs in North America
2. /market segments AI accounting software for SMBs in North America
3. /market demand AI accounting software for SMBs in North America
4. /market landscape AI accounting software for SMBs in North America
5. /market whitespace AI accounting software for SMBs in North America
6. /market regulatory AI accounting software for SMBs in North America
7. /market launch AI accounting software for SMB finance teams

## What To Say While Running It

- "We start with market size so we do not build in a weak category."
- "Then we choose the best first segment to win."
- "Next we validate if demand is real now, not just hype."
- "Then we map competitors and find underserved whitespace."
- "Finally we de-risk with regulatory checks and generate a launch plan."

## Deep Analysis Note

For deep command analysis, Brief now simulates 20 to 30 research passes before final output, so results feel like multi-round analyst work.
