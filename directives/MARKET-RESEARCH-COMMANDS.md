# Brief – Added Deep Market Research Commands

This file documents the six new market-research-focused commands added to balance the existing marketing-heavy set.

## New Commands

1. `/market sizing <topic>`

- Purpose: Estimate TAM/SAM/SOM with assumptions and scenarios.
- Output: `MARKET-SIZING.md`

2. `/market segments <topic>`

- Purpose: Build segment map by jobs-to-be-done, urgency, and spend potential.
- Output: `MARKET-SEGMENTS.md`

3. `/market demand <topic>`

- Purpose: Validate demand from intent signals, trend velocity, and buyer behavior.
- Output: `DEMAND-VALIDATION.md`

4. `/market landscape <topic>`

- Purpose: Map ecosystem players, substitutes, and strategic cluster positioning.
- Output: `INDUSTRY-LANDSCAPE.md`

5. `/market whitespace <topic>`

- Purpose: Identify underserved opportunity spaces and rank by monetization potential.
- Output: `WHITESPACE-OPPORTUNITIES.md`

6. `/market regulatory <topic>`

- Purpose: Evaluate regulatory/compliance constraints and launch risk by jurisdiction.
- Output: `REGULATORY-RISK-SCAN.md`

## Why These Close The Gap

- Existing commands were strong on growth execution (copy, ads, social, funnel, landing).
- These additions increase strategic market intelligence depth:
  - market size realism
  - segment economics
  - demand validation
  - industry structure mapping
  - whitespace opportunity discovery
  - policy/regulatory feasibility
